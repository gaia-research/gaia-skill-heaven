## The rule that matters most

**The person makes every decision in the proof.** You prepare the run, the person
runs `/skill-ultra <goal>`, approves or edits in the decisions step, and answers the
incidents, in a terminal that **no agent can type into**: outside herdr and tmux, with
no opt-in env var set. No agent, fixture or pane keystroke may produce an operator
entry in the proof trace. If the trace shows `multiplexed: true` or `optIn: true` on
any operator entry, the run is not the proof: record it and stop. You never fill in
a missing step and never describe one that did not happen (invariant 15).

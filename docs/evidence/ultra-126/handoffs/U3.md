## Implementation summary

- **Proposal & charter validation (`ops/propose.ts`, `store.ts`):** Refuses any proposal when no recon record exists, when `reconId` is missing/empty/unknown, when any `arsenal`, `queue`, or `allowedSkills` entry does not trace to a candidate card in a `matched` gap of the cited recon record (INV-U1, K1), or when any of the PR-4 bounds (`recoverLimit`, `reopenLimit`, `sessionRetention`) is omitted (no compiled default). Carries the PR-6 authority split template (`CHARTER_AUTHORITY_SPLIT_TEMPLATE`), marks human-led skills `convergenceOnly: true` (FD-3), and persists `charter.json` bound by `charter-sha256:r<revision>:<sha256>` in `trace.jsonl` and `artifactSha256` in `evidence.jsonl`.
- **MCP elicitation operator channel (`operator.ts`, `engine.ts`):** Uses MCP elicitation (`channel: "mcp-elicitation"`, `attribution: "terminal-input, unauthenticated"`), embedding the full server-generated control strategy directly in the elicitation prompt. `cancel` and `decline` record no decision and leave the campaign in `DECISIONS`. Mints U0a's `OperatorEventCapability` only on elicitation `accept`. Fails closed by default when `HERDR_ENV`, `HERDR_SOCKET_PATH`, or `TMUX` is present in the server environment (FD-11 / D-010); under the unadvertised opt-in env var (defined only in `vocabulary.ts`), requires an exact match against `OPERATOR_MULTIPLEXED_OPT_IN_VALUE` (no `.trim().toLowerCase()`, no `"1"`/`"true"`, preserving deliberate FD-11 friction), records `optIn: true` on every operator entry, surfaces `optIn: true` in render and status without naming the env var, and requires typing the presented charter `shortDigest`. Candidate amendments from `options.amendment` are pre-rendered in full and require confirming the presented candidate digest; if operator form edits produce a materially different resulting charter, a confirmation elicitation presents the resulting merged charter and requires confirming its resulting `shortDigest` before committing revision `n+1` (preserving `queue`).
- **Control-strategy renderer & `/skill-ultra` (`render.ts`, `render-ladder.mjs`, `skill-ultra.md`):** Deterministically renders phases, arsenal by phase, allowed skills, queue, clauses, automatic vs. human-only authority split, recovery bounds, pause window, checkpoints, stops, absent lenses (never as `0`), D-009 attribution and multiplexer flags, headless `-p` explanation (plan §5.9), and P1d per-permission-mode findings (`bypass`, `auto`, `default`/`manual`). Bare `/skill-ultra` stays unchanged (PR-7) and all Ultra copy remains `"provisioned"`.
- **Bundled MCP & `gaia-research` delta (`mcp/server.ts`, `gaia-research#336`):** Adds the one FD-1 instruction clause alongside `/skill-hell`, enables `campaign` by default in `createSkillSummonMcpServer` (lifting D-002's gate), and ships the companion `gaia-research` PR (#336) adding `N14` (FD-7) and the FD-6 lexicon delta (`decisions`, `converge`, `explore`, and the interchangeability triples).

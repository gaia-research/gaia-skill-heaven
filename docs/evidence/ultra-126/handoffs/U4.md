## Implementation summary

- **Work units, bounds, and queue (`units.ts`, `queue.ts`, `ops/{next,report,checkpoint}.ts`):** Enforces the full U4 work-unit state graph purely with charter-supplied `recoverLimit` and `reopenLimit` (no compiled defaults, supporting `0`). `next` orders ready units whose dependencies are `done` by dependency depth, charter phase order, and stable tie-break without stalling independent units when others are `blocked` or `needs-human` (PR-9). Regrouping before decisions updates batches without a checkpoint; regrouping after decisions writes an atomic `CHECKPOINT` entry alongside `units.json`.
- **Receipts & INV-U15 (`receipts.ts`, `engine.ts`, `ops/report.ts`):** Every verify receipt binds `(unitId, command, outputSha256, exitCode)` with `status: "attested"` and zero quality-endorsement prose. Unit closure (`closesUnit: true`) is rejected at both `ops/report.ts` and pure `engine.ts` unless the command is explicitly named in `charter.acceptanceCommands` (or `open`-committed `acceptanceCommands`).
- **Steering wiring & retrieval isolation (`engine.ts`, `ops/report.ts`):** The engine privately owns the `steering.ts` runtime adapter: verify failures emit `behavioral-failure` and verify passes emit `behavioral-recovery` through `stepSteering`. Bounds exhaustion emits `convergenceIncident` (`requiresPerson: true`) on `E.verify` to enter `CONVERGENCE` while stepping steering. Retrieval-derived payloads (`score`, `margin`, `noMatch`, `matchKind`, `retrieval`, `ranking`, `classified`, `nameMatchesQuery`, `filtered`, `cards`, `previewed`) are rejected before steering/unit state, and `retrieval-refusal` records `HOLD` without exploring harder or incrementing retry counts.
- **Transactional store & crash recovery (`store.ts`):** All `units.json` and `receipts.jsonl` updates are staged (`.units.stage.json`, `.receipts.stage.jsonl`, `.tx.stage.json`) and committed inside `CampaignStore.#appendSerialized` with bidirectional `unitsSha256` binding between `trace.jsonl` and `evidence.jsonl`. Interrupted writes across pre-trace, post-trace/pre-evidence, and post-evidence/pre-rename boundaries reconcile deterministically for both agent and privileged ops, while trace-only tampering or missing/modified artifacts fail closed into `FAILED_SAFE`.
- **Receipt replay CLI (`cli.ts`):** `skill-zero --campaign-replay-receipts <trace>` verifies the trace and evidence commitments, requires and matches sibling `receipts.jsonl` against accepted `E.verify` trace events before executing any command, re-runs commands only on explicit human invocation, upgrades matching receipts to `"replayed"` while leaving mismatched ones `"attested"`, and leaves `trace.jsonl` / `evidence.jsonl` unmodified so `--campaign-verify` remains green.

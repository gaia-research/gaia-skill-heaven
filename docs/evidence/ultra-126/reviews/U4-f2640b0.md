## Findings

- `packages/core/src/cli.ts:429` - **critical, blocking:** Replay executes every command in a sibling `receipts.jsonl` after checking only its shape. Those receipts are not compared with the verified trace’s receipt-bearing events. A modified or substituted sidecar can therefore cause an explicitly invoked replay of a valid trace to execute unrelated shell commands. Bind each replayed receipt to an accepted, verified trace event before executing anything; reject missing, extra, or mismatched sidecar entries.

- `packages/core/src/campaign/engine.ts:155` - **critical, blocking:** When a charter has no `acceptanceCommands`, the engine accepts any passing receipt as authority to close a unit. That violates INV-U15: the command must be named by the charter. Reject closure when no charter-named command exists, including for direct engine events and trace verification.

- `packages/skill-summon/src/campaign/store.ts:829` - **critical, blocking:** `units.json` is accepted based on shape alone, and a missing file is treated as an uninitialized queue. Deleting it after work has started can reset unit states and recovery counts; a validly shaped replacement can change bounds or mark units done without the corresponding verified transitions. Bind the queue to the verified campaign history and charter, and fail safe on missing or inconsistent persisted state once initialized.

- `packages/skill-summon/src/campaign/ops/report.ts:400` - **critical, blocking:** A verification report persists the trace, queue, and receipt in three separate operations. A failure or interruption after the trace append can leave the campaign’s completed count or steering advanced while the unit remains active and the receipt is absent; a retry can count the same unit again. Persist these changes transactionally, or stage and reconcile them with fail-safe recovery. The analogous queue-write-before-checkpoint sequence at `ops/checkpoint.ts:197` can leave post-decision regrouping without its required checkpoint.

- `packages/skill-summon/src/campaign/ops/report.ts:390` - **warning, blocking:** Exhausting the recovery and reopen bounds returns a `convergence` decision and moves the unit to `needs-human`, but only a verify event is appended. The campaign engine therefore remains in `executing`, rather than entering its convergence state; the tests assert the returned decision but not the persisted campaign state. Wire the exhausted-bounds result into the campaign convergence transition and test it after reload.

- `packages/skill-summon/src/campaign/operator.ts:253` — **critical**: The authoritative elicitation prompt shows only the goal, revision, and digest—not the control strategy, permissions, bounds, queue, or stops being approved. The full render is returned through the model’s tool path, so a misleading model summary can lead a person to approve a materially different charter. Present the server-generated strategy, or an equivalent complete reviewable summary bound to the digest, directly through the operator channel before accepting approval.

- `packages/skill-summon/src/campaign/operator.ts:957` — **critical**: Amendment fields supplied by the model in the tool call are merged into the accepted decision after elicitation, while the form neither shows those fields nor asks the person to enter them. For example, a request to amend can silently change bounds, goal, or authority text; the trace then attributes the amendment to the operator. Obtain amendment contents through the operator channel or display and confirm the exact proposed amendment and its resulting digest before recording the operator event.

- `packages/skill-summon/src/campaign/tool.ts:30` — **warning**: The advertised MCP tool description and schema still describe only `open`, `recon`, and `status`. They omit `propose`, `decide`, and `amend`, as well as proposal fields such as arsenal citations and required bounds. Clients and models using tool discovery cannot reliably construct the newly supported workflow; strict schema-driven clients may discard or reject its necessary fields. Publish the operations and their input fields in the tool schema and rebuild the bundle.

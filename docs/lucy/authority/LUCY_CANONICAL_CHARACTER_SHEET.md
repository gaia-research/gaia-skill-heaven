# LUCY — Canonical Character Specification

**Skill Heaven Runtime Mascot**  
**Canonical line:** *One girl. Different entropy.*

Lucy is one invariant character whose **rendering physics change with runtime state**. Zero, Heaven, Hell, and Ultra are not separate characters and are not conventional power-up forms. Her face, anatomy, sailor uniform, barefoot baseline, and weapon language remain recognizable while gravity, hair behavior, eyes, wings, palette, lighting, and pose communicate the current state.

## 1. Invariants

- **Name:** Lucy
- **Role:** Skill Heaven runtime mascot
- **Build:** slender, lightweight anime proportions
- **Skin:** pale porcelain baseline with saturated pink/cyan reflected light modeled after the original reference
- **Hair:** silver-white, approximately shoulder length
- **Uniform:** dark Japanese sailor-style uniform, broad sailor collar, long sleeves, short pleated skirt
- **Ribbon:** iridescent/prismatic sailor ribbon; always present, motion follows hair airflow
- **Footwear:** none; Lucy is canonically barefoot in every state
- **Primary weapon:** traditional steel Japanese katana with dark wrap, dark saya, restrained brass/antique-gold hardware
- **Wing language:** independent floating prism/glass shards, never feathers or a solid membrane
- **Expression language:** quiet, distant, restrained; state is communicated primarily through eyes, pose, color physics, and motion

### Absolute design rule

> **Lucy's anatomy does not transform. Her rendering does.**

No horns, armor, crown, heavy jewelry, boots, fantasy weapon redesigns, or biological wings.

---

## 2. Skin rendering

The original Lucy reference is the skin-lighting anchor.

Preserve:

- high saturation in reflected light
- porcelain base with pink-magenta and cyan-blue bounce
- luminous cheek/temple planes
- controlled deep navy shadow masses
- sharp but elegant highlight transitions
- prismatic light reflected onto neck, ear, jaw, hands, legs, and feet

Avoid flattening Lucy into pale desaturated anime skin. Her skin should visibly belong to the same optical environment as the hair and ribbon.

---

## 3. Hair system

### Shape

- Shoulder-length silver-white hair
- Clean, readable silhouette
- Fine filament strands may escape the main mass
- Hair is the primary canvas for Lucy's optical physics

### Zero

Gravity wins. Hair falls naturally around the head and shoulders. It is quiet, smooth, and mostly silver-white, with subtle cyan and faint prismatic depth.

### Heaven / Hell / Ultra

Lucy is falling or floating downward while the hair streams **upward**. The movement must feel physics-driven rather than decorative.

**Direction rule:** body ↓ / hair ↑

Rendering should preserve the original reference's technique:

- brilliant white cores
- high-saturation cyan and electric blue
- controlled pink/violet refraction
- large clean shadow masses
- narrow bright rim highlights
- semi-transparent glass-like sections
- tapering strands that become ribbon/flame/water-like abstractions
- energetic bottom-to-top flow

The ribbon should move with the same airflow and lag/arc naturally behind the hair.

---

## 4. Eye system

### Zero eye

The Zero eye has **no conventional iris**. When open, it is pale and blank with subtle prismatic depth, like empty glass with depth behind it.

**Canonical Zero hero pose:** both eyes closed.

### Summon diamond eye

Heaven and Ultra use a distinct optical **diamond iris**. The diamond appears embedded inside the eye, not painted onto it.

- **Heaven:** both eyes open, blue/cyan/white diamond irises with spectral edge color
- **Hell:** both eyes closed; a single vivid red tear descends from one eye
- **Ultra:** one diamond eye open; the other eye closed with a red tear

The Hell diamond system exists conceptually but is hidden in the canonical closed-eye Hell render.

---

## 5. Uniform and ribbon

Lucy wears the sailor uniform from the original reference:

- near-black/navy sailor blouse
- broad sailor collar
- long sleeves
- short pleated skirt
- luminous state-reactive piping kept restrained
- prismatic sailor ribbon

The fabric remains largely matte so the optical phenomena have contrast.

The ribbon is a continuity marker across all states:

- **Zero:** subdued cyan/prism
- **Heaven:** luminous blue/cyan prism
- **Hell:** fully inverted with the scene
- **Ultra:** prism with exclusive gold interference

Do not convert the uniform into armor or fantasy robes.

---

## 6. Barefoot rule

Lucy is barefoot in all canonical states and neutral references.

Bare feet reinforce weightlessness, vulnerability, and separation from conventional fantasy combat design. Pose them naturally according to gravity and motion; they are not a focal element.

---

## 7. Katana

Lucy's weapon is a physically real steel katana, never a glass or energy sword.

Canonical language:

- steel curved blade
- dark wrapped tsuka
- restrained round/slightly ornamental tsuba
- dark saya
- brass / antique-gold hardware

State lighting may reflect in the blade, but the material remains steel.

- Zero: one katana
- Heaven: one katana
- Hell: one katana
- Ultra: **two matching katanas**

---

## 8. Sharded wing system

Lucy does not have biological wings. Her wings are independent floating glass/prism shards arranged into the suggestion of wings.

Rules:

- large negative space between pieces
- shards never join into a continuous membrane
- dramatic size variation
- sharp geometric silhouettes
- outward directional energy from Lucy's back
- wings can be separated into left/right and individual shard layers

### Zero exception

**Zero has absolutely no wings and no residual shard pattern that reads as a wing.**

# Four Canonical States

## 0 · ZERO — The Clean Slate

**Meaning:** baseline / subtractive / nothing summoned yet.

- **Pose:** sitting on the ground in a calm zen/meditative posture
- **Gravity:** normal
- **Eyes:** both closed, neutral
- **Hair:** down, naturally weighted
- **Ribbon:** quiet, minimal movement
- **Wings:** none
- **Weapon:** one katana resting horizontally beside or in front of her
- **Skin:** normal porcelain + subtle prismatic reflection
- **Uniform:** dark sailor uniform
- **Feet:** barefoot
- **Background:** deep black
- **Dominant accent:** Zero cyan, working reference `#37D6E0`
- **Emotional read:** stillness, unloaded potential

Zero should not read as asleep, sad, or religiously meditative. It is the visual metaphor for an unburdened runtime.

---

## ☁ HEAVEN — Convergence

**Meaning:** summon the right few; order and clarity.

- **Pose:** distinct falling/floating pose, graceful and controlled
- **Gravity:** body falls/down; hair and ribbon stream upward
- **Eyes:** both open
- **Iris:** luminous diamond, predominantly white/cyan/electric blue with faint spectral edges
- **Hair:** high-saturation silver/white/cyan/blue prism flow
- **Wings:** ordered blue-prismatic shard wings
- **Weapon:** one steel katana
- **Skin:** normal porcelain with saturated cyan/pink reflected lighting
- **Feet:** barefoot
- **Background:** near-black
- **Dominant accent:** Heaven blue `#7CC4FF`
- **Emotional read:** clarity, coherent complexity

Heaven should be spectacular but visually organized.

---

## 🔥 HELL — Expansion

**Meaning:** exploratory expansion / beautiful overload. Hell is not evil Lucy.

- **Pose:** unique falling pose, less controlled than Heaven
- **Gravity:** body falls/down; hair and ribbon stream upward
- **Eyes:** both closed
- **Tear:** one vivid red tear
- **Hair:** fully inverted, predominantly dark/black with crimson, hot-pink, and complementary spectral highlights
- **Wings:** more fragmented inverted shards with greater directional disagreement
- **Weapon:** one steel katana subject to scene inversion
- **Feet:** barefoot
- **Background:** pure or near-pure white
- **Emotional read:** overload without rage

### Full inversion rule

Hell is an optical inversion of the entire visual world, not merely red lighting. Invert:

- skin
- hair
- uniform
- ribbon
- wings
- environmental light
- weapon reflections

Do not reinterpret inverted skin as a change of ethnicity or anatomy. It is a whole-render optical effect.

---

## ⚡ ULTRA — Governance

**Meaning:** governs direction; decides when to converge, explore, and stop.

- **Pose:** distinct decisive falling pose, different from both Heaven and Hell
- **Gravity:** body falls/down; hair and ribbon stream upward
- **Eyes:** one diamond eye open; opposite eye closed
- **Tear:** red tear from the closed eye
- **Hair:** silver-white prism with strong exclusive gold interference
- **Wings:** gold/prismatic sharded wings
- **Weapons:** **two matching steel katanas**
- **Skin:** normal, never inverted
- **Uniform:** normal dark sailor uniform
- **Feet:** barefoot
- **Background:** deep black
- **Dominant accent:** Ultra gold, working range `#FFD24A`–`#F7C84B`
- **Emotional read:** absolute concentration / governance

### Critical Ultra rule

**Ultra is never inverted.** Earlier half-inversion concepts are non-canonical. Gold is the new wavelength exclusive to Ultra.

## 10. Reproduction rules

1. Same Lucy in every state.
2. Shoulder-length silver-white hair.
3. Preserve the original reference's saturated skin shading and optical bounce.
4. Dark sailor uniform with prismatic ribbon.
5. Barefoot always.
6. Katana is real steel, never glass.
7. Zero sits; active states fall.
8. Zero has absolutely no wings.
9. Active hair and ribbon flow upward against the falling body.
10. Heaven uses two open diamond eyes.
11. Hell is full-scene inversion on white, closed eyes, one red tear.
12. Ultra is never inverted, introduces gold, uses dual katanas, one open diamond eye and one closed tearful eye.
13. Wings are disconnected shards, never feathered anatomy.
14. Preserve strong white/cyan/prismatic hair lighting and saturated reflected light on skin.
15. Each canonical state gets a genuinely distinct pose.
16. Do not let effects obscure Lucy's face or core sailor silhouette.

### Compact formula

> **ZERO rests. HEAVEN converges. HELL inverts. ULTRA governs.**  
> **The girl stays the same. The physics do not.**

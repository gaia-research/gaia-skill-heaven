## Contract and result

The model was asked to change only both eyes to naturally closed eyelids and to
add exactly one vivid red screen-left tear. The returned crop was not allowed to
alter the full master directly. `scripts/assets/lucy-v2-export.py` resizes the
patch to the fixed source crop and composites it solely through an antialiased
two-eyes-plus-one-tear mask. The final Hell master retains the accepted Heaven
alpha, dimensions, coordinates, silhouette, pose, anatomy, sword, uniform,
hair, ribbon, wing shards, and crop byte-for-layout.

`scripts/assets/lucy-v2-validate.py` reports **0** differing foreground pixels
outside that declared mask when compared with exact unpremultiplied RGB
inversion. Skin participates in the inversion; it is not preserved or graded.
